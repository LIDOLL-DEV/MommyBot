GENTLE CARE

Diaper checks

Every two to four hours MommyBot picks one LiDollID-verified member of this role, taking turns so everyone is asked before anyone twice, and asks in this channel whether their diaper is still dry. She believes whatever they answer. No questions are asked between 22:00 and 06:00 server time. The channel must not be visible to @everyone, and the role is the opt-in: remove it to stop being asked.
