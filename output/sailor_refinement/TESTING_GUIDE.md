# Outfit pack notes

The builder validates eight unique sheets, 128 nonempty frames, binary alpha, and grayscale. Each short dress must expose at least 35% of the source diaper's opaque pixels in every frame. validation.json records counts for all three styles. The sailor skirt must retain the original dress alpha mask below row 23, with trim following the animated lower edge. Check visual motion, hands, hems, and final GameMaker sprite origins separately.
